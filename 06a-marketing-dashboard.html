LUCIA — what the marketing director takes to the CMO

The headline: Lucia delivered 3,142 qualified leads in Q2 FY26 versus 988 in the equivalent pre-Lucia quarter — at the same paid-media spend. Lead-to-meeting conversion went from 14% to 38%. Quote-to-close ratio multiplied 2.7×. Net influenced pipeline is +$11.4M, conservatively attributed.

The cost story: Cost per qualified lead dropped 74%, from $184 to $48. Service-ticket deflection saved ~1,840 tickets/quarter (real cost reduction in the support org, not just marketing). Discovery Center bookings up — a previously high-friction capability now happens in-chat.

What's next: Pillar 6B (voice-of-customer insights) is what's driving the FY27 content roadmap — turns out, ~14% of all chat sessions ended at a question Lucia couldn't answer well, which means a ~$1.6M annual content investment with a clear, measurable target.
