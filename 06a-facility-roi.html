LUCIA — the case Elena makes to the dean and to NIH

The headline: the Imaging Core enabled 47 more publications, captured $28M in new sponsored research, and recovered $1.4M of additional indirect cost — at the same staffing level as last year. The platform did the work that an additional 4–5 FTE staff would have done, at less than half the cost.

The asymmetry NIH should care about: shared instrumentation grants (S10, P30) score on usage breadth, training, and outputs. All three are up sharply this year — 612 users vs 489, onboarding time cut 60%, 142 publications credited. The case for the next P30 renewal writes itself.

Where Elena would still spend money: Lucia integration for the Zeiss + Leica + Nikon instruments would close the last 35% of facility throughput sitting outside the platform. That's a 2026 capital request — Lucia handles the business case math when the budget cycle opens.
