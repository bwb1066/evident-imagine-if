Methods · auto-drafted from your protocol

Two-photon calcium imaging

Acute brain slices were prepared from male and female SST-Cre × Ai14 mice (P28–P35) crossed with the Thy1-GCaMP6f line. Slices (350 µm) were prepared in ice-cold ACSF containing (in mM): 124 NaCl, 2.5 KCl, 1.25 NaH₂PO₄, 24 NaHCO₃, 12.5 glucose, 1 MgSO₄, and 2 CaCl₂ (pH 7.4, 305 mOsm), and allowed to recover for ≥1 h at 32°C before imaging.

Two-photon imaging was performed on an Evident FLUOVIEW FV5000 with an InSight X3+ laser tuned to 920 nm. Layer 2/3 of primary somatosensory cortex was imaged using a 25× water-immersion objective (NA 1.10). Acquisition parameters: 256 × 256 pixels, resonance galvano at 30 Hz, 0.42 µs pixel dwell, 10 mW at sample for GCaMP6f and 14 mW for jGCaMP8s (Channel 1: 495–540 nm GaAsP HSD; Channel 2: 575–630 nm GaAsP HSD). Photobleach budget was managed automatically using the Lucia acquisition assistant (Evident, 2026), with hard limit set at 30 min continuous imaging per slice.

Image segmentation was performed using TruAI's pre-trained SST-pyramidal model (v2.3) embedded in cellSens. ΔF/F event detection used a 3σ threshold with minimum event duration of 60 ms. All sessions were quality-controlled by Lucia for focus drift, photobleach decay, and signal stability; sessions failing QC were excluded (n=4 of 89, 4.5%).

Burst- versus tonic-firing classification was performed using a feature-vector k-means clustering on inter-event interval distributions. Statistical comparisons used two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests (R 4.3.2) for non-parametric distributions, with Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons. Effect sizes reported as Cliff's δ. Sample sizes: 89 burst, 547 tonic from N=12 animals across 28 slices.
