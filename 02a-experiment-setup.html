LUCIA — what I optimized for

Your stated goal: compare signal dynamics between GCaMP6f and the faster jGCaMP8s in the same SST+ population. The key tradeoff is photobleach vs SNR — jGCaMP8s is dimmer at rest, so you'll want enough laser power to resolve baseline cleanly, but not so much that you photobleach the GCaMP6f comparison slices over the multi-week campaign.

What I drafted: a single protocol that works for both indicators with one calibration step per session. Photobleach budget calculated against your historical FV5000 use. Speed set to capture the 8s indicator's faster off-kinetics. Three preset variants for different slice depths.

LUCIA — three calls I'd want you to make before we save this

Stimulation epochs (line 11). Your stim protocol from 2024 used 4 epochs of paired light + sound. I kept those defaults but jGCaMP8s decays faster — you might want a longer inter-epoch interval (currently 8s, suggesting 12s) to ensure baseline return. Your call.

Calibration overhead (line 14). I added 12 minutes/session for PSF + autofluor. Across 80 slices that's ~16 hours of total scope time. Worth it for between-week comparability, but it does slow you down. If you'd rather skip and rely on post-hoc normalization, I can re-draft.

Photobleach hard limit (line 16). 30 min/slice is conservative for both indicators. If you sometimes go over for particularly interesting slices, I can make it a soft limit (Lucia warns, doesn't pause) instead.
