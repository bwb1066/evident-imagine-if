LUCIA — what to do with this

This is the bit traditional analytics never showed. Pageview reports tell you which pages got traffic. Form analytics tell you which forms converted. Neither tells you what the visitor was actually trying to ask. Lucia's chat logs are a direct ear into customer intent in customer language.

The four content gaps are the FY27 marketing roadmap. Each is sized — by session volume, by downstream pipeline impact, and by content-investment cost. The super-resolution positioning gap alone is worth $840K of at-risk pipeline. Closing all four is roughly a 2-FTE quarter of content work for what Lucia estimates is ~$1.6M of pipeline recovery.

The terminology mismatches are SEO money on the floor. 6 simple word additions to existing product pages — "slide scanner," "AI microscope," "benchtop," etc. — would meaningfully improve Evident's organic AI-search and Google ranking on the queries actual buyers type. None of these require new content; just edits to titles and meta descriptions.
