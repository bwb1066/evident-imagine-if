LUCIA — what this lets the consortium do

Replication-built-in. Most multi-lab efforts in cellular neuroscience either fail to replicate (different protocols, hidden drift) or never get published because nobody can defend the normalization. Lucia's protocol-fidelity tracking and per-site calibration corrections solve both — every claim in the paper has a defensible cross-site provenance.

Trust between PIs. The four PIs don't have to debate whether Lab X's data is comparable to Lab Y's. Lucia surfaces the calibration math openly. Disagreements stay scientific, not procedural.

The bigger picture. This protocol is now packaged. Any new lab joining the consortium can re-run on their FV5000 with one calibration session — Lucia handles the normalization against the consortium baseline automatically. Replication doesn't have to be a heroic effort anymore.
