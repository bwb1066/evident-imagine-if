LUCIA — what makes this comparison defensible

Every claim is sourced. The FV5000 numbers come from evidentscientific.com/products/confocal/fv5000. The Zeiss LSM 980 and Leica STELLARIS 8 numbers come from each vendor's own published product pages — Lucia ingested them at indexing time and refreshes monthly. No third-party claims, no Evident inventing competitor weaknesses.

Lucia tells the truth where competitors are stronger. Airyscan 2 super-resolution is genuinely industry-leading; Lucia says so. TauContrast FLIM is genuinely a Leica strength; Lucia says so. The willingness to acknowledge competitor strengths is what makes the Evident-favoring conclusions trustworthy on the dimensions where they're true.

What Lucia won't claim. Performance comparisons that depend on the user's specific sample, throughput claims that depend on operator skill, or any number not published by the vendors themselves. When a buyer asks "which is faster on my actual application," Lucia hands them off to an applications specialist rather than guessing.
