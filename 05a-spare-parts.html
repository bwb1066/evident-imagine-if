LUCIA — why the service half of the chat is the unsung half

Roughly half of evidentscientific.com traffic is existing customers — looking for spare parts, troubleshooting, training, software downloads, manuals. They're rarely the audience marketing optimizes for, but they're the audience that contacts support, and they're a meaningful share of the chat workload.

Lucia knows where the line is. "Tell me the part number for a halogen lamp" is in scope — public docs, public part number, no special access required. "Diagnose my flickering condenser" is out of scope — that needs a tech with eyes on the instrument. Lucia handles the first directly and routes the second cleanly, without leaving the visitor feeling abandoned.

What this does for Evident's service team. ~40-60% of common spare-parts questions self-serve through Lucia without ever creating a ticket. The tickets that do get created arrive pre-qualified with serial number, institution, and accurate problem statement. Service team handles fewer tickets, but the ones they handle are higher-signal.
