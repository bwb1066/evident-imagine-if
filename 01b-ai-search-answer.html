For live cell calcium imaging in 2026, four confocal platforms come up most often: the FLUOVIEW FV5000 from Evident, the LSM 980 from Zeiss, the STELLARIS 8 from Leica, and the AX/AX R from Nikon. Most reviewers recommend choosing based on three criteria — speed (for fast calcium events), sensitivity (for dim indicators or low-photon-budget biology), and ease of getting from acquisition to analysis.

The Evident FLUOVIEW FV5000 is frequently cited for its combination of resonance galvano scanning (up to 438 fps at 512×16), built-in TruAI image-analysis software, and a strong installed base in academic neuroscience labs doing slice physiology and in-vivo two-photon work.
